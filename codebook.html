How the data were built

Every step reads only files whose fingerprint (SHA-256) matches the one recorded when the file was downloaded or made. A step stops if any input does not match, and it never overwrites a file.

  1. Download. 01_download_acs.sh pulls American Community Survey 5-year tables from the Census Data API for Mississippi and Tennessee school districts. The dashboard uses the 2020-2024 estimates. Other downloads: Census TIGER/Line 2024 school district boundaries (04_download_tiger.sh), Tennessee district profiles (05_download_state_files.sh, saved from a browser when the site refused scripted downloads), and, saved from a browser, the Mississippi enrollment file, the Mississippi school meals (CEP) list, the NCES directory, and USDA's SNAP-Authorized Retailer Access Map.
  2. Clean. 10_build_acs_long.R turns each raw ACS file into a long table with one row per district and variable: the estimate, its 90% margin of error, and any Census special code (D067).
  3. Build. Stata 19 do-files make the dashboard's data (D067):
    • 30_dashboard_districts.do: the 277 districts, their IDs, K-12 enrollment, and who the state counts now.
    • 31_dashboard_indicators.do: 17 census indicators with margins of error.
    • 32_dashboard_map.do: the simplified district shapes.
    • 33_dashboard_food.do: the food access indicator.
    • 34_dashboard_neighbors.do: which districts border each other.
  4. Check. Each product was recomputed separately in Python, cell by cell, before it went on the dashboard.
  5. Show. The dashboard's files in data/ are exact copies of those products. The index, re-sharing and dollars are worked out in the browser (D071).

Districts and IDs

The dashboard shows the school systems each state's formula pays (D063, D068).

  • Mississippi: 137 unified (K-12) districts.
  • Tennessee: 140. That is 124 unified districts plus 16 city K-8 systems. The Census also draws 16 "secondary" pieces: county high-school areas inside those cities. Their students are funded through the county system, so they are not shown separately. A county system is described by the census numbers for its own K-12 area only.
  • Left off: Fort Campbell Schools (4700078). It is a census district but is not in Tennessee's district profile and is not funded by TISA.
  • Census figures describe everyone living in a district's area, not the students enrolled in it.

Margins of error

The ACS is a survey, so every estimate has a margin of error. Each _moe column is the 90% margin of error: the true value is likely within that many points of the estimate. Small districts often have wide margins.

  • Published percents: the Census margin of error as published.
  • 100 minus a published percent (no broadband, adults without a diploma): keeps the published margin of error.
  • Percents we built from counts use the Census Bureau's formulas from Understanding and Using American Community Survey Data:
    • sum or difference of counts: the square root of the sum of each margin of error squared
    • a share p = numerator / denominator: the square root of (numerator margin squared minus p squared times denominator margin squared), divided by the denominator
    • if the number under that square root is negative, the ratio formula is used instead: plus in place of minus. This happened for rent burden in 9 districts.
  • Food access has no margin of error. USDA publishes none.

Decision: D069.

Indicators

Each indicator has two columns: _est (the value) and _moe (its margin of error). Seventeen are from the ACS 2020-2024 5-year estimates (D069). Food access is from USDA (D073). Higher means more need for every indicator except median household income, which is flipped so lower income counts as more need. Groups follow the dashboard's domains (D072, D073).

Map and neighbors

  • Map shapes (data/map.geojson): the 277 districts from the 2024 TIGER/Line unified and elementary files. Each boundary is simplified on its own to about 200 meters (0.002 degrees) and coordinates are rounded to 4 decimals, so shared borders can differ slightly up close. Fort Campbell shows as a gap. Shapes only, no data values. Decision D070.
  • Neighbors (data/neighbors.csv): two districts border each other if they are in the same state and share at least 0.00001 degrees (about 1 meter) of border in the full 2024 TIGER/Line boundaries. Touching only at a corner does not count. Built from the full boundaries because the simplified map shapes gave 16 wrong pairs out of 655. 655 pairs, and every district has at least one neighbor. Decision D074.
  • Tract-to-district shares (used for food access): the team's 2024 crosswalk, built from 2020 Census blocks. For each tract and district, the share of the tract's people living in that district. Every tract with people adds up to 1 across districts. Decision D073.
